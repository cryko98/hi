How a Puppy Got His Halo

In the Beginning…

Book of Woof · 1:1

High above the clouds, past the gates where the good boys go, there lived a tiny shiba with paws too big for his body and a heart too big for the sky. The angels called him Inu. He never once chewed a slipper. He never barked at the mailman. He was, by every heavenly measure, the goodest boy who had ever lived.

So one sunny morning, the clouds parted and a golden halo floated down and settled right above his fluffy ears. It hummed. It glowed. It fit perfectly.

But Inu wasn't interested in floating around on clouds all day. Every time he peeked over the edge, he saw them: the degens of Solana. Rugged. Dumped on. Staring at red candles at 3 AM. Crying into their cold coffee.

Inu tilted his head. He knew exactly what they needed.

So he jumped.

I The Descent

Holy Inu tumbled through a hundred clouds and landed — paws first — right on the Solana chain. The halo stayed on. It always stays on.

II The First Blessing

He found a degen who'd been rugged six times that week. Inu sat down next to him and simply said "hi." The degen's portfolio didn't change — but his spirit did. That was the first $HI.

III The Fair Launch

No VCs in the clouds. No presale behind the pearly gates. Holy Inu came down the way every honest pup should: a fair launch on pump.fun, open to every soul at once.

IV The Congregation

Now the faithful gather. They change their pfps, they light up their halos, and every morning they greet each other the only way that matters: Say $HI.
